How do I take a reading?

Scene mode reads the whole frame: aim at the area your camera will see. For the most precise number, switch to Card mode and fill the frame with a white or grey card held where your subject stands.

Why calibrate?

Every camera sees color a little differently, and so does the iPhone. Calibrate once: point your iPhone and your camera at the same white wall, enter what your camera reads, and ColorMeter remembers the difference. From then on every reading is matched to your camera. You can recalibrate, adjust by hand, or add more cameras any time in Settings → Calibration.

Can I use it without calibrating?

Yes. Skip calibration and ColorMeter shows the iPhone’s own reading. Calibrating simply makes the number match your camera.

What do Lock and Save do?

Lock freezes the current reading so you can walk to your camera and dial it in. Save keeps the reading in History with a location, an optional note and the time, so every room and every setup is remembered. Swipe a saved reading to delete it, or open it to share.

The reading jumps around

Mixed light (window + tungsten, practicals, LED walls) really does change across the room. Aim at where your subject is, use Card mode, or set Response to Steady in Settings for a calmer number.

The camera view is black

ColorMeter needs camera access. Open iOS Settings → ColorMeter and turn on Camera.
