PRIVACY POLICY

Nothing leaves your iPhone

ColorMeter does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Effective November 6, 2026 · Applies to ColorMeter for iPhone

Camera

ColorMeter uses the camera only to measure the color temperature (Kelvin) and tint of the light in front of it. Frames are analysed on the device, in real time, and are discarded immediately. ColorMeter never saves, records or uploads photos or video.

What is stored on your iPhone

Your settings, camera calibrations (for example “FX3 · +250K”) and the readings you choose to save are kept locally on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a saved reading, iOS opens the standard share sheet and you decide where the text goes. ColorMeter does not see or keep a copy.

Third parties

ColorMeter contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, crash reporting or advertising frameworks, and it does not connect to the internet. Apple may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous App Store and crash statistics if you have chosen to share them with developers in iOS Settings; that is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

ColorMeter does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
